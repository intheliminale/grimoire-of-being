Major Arcana · II
The High Priestess
Moon in Pisces · 2nd House
Your Moon Card
The keeper of hidden knowledge, intuitive wisdom, and the veil between worlds. She does not explain herself. She knows, and that is enough. She is the silence that contains more truth than any speech.
Your Moon in Pisces IS the High Priestess — intuitive beyond explanation, psychically porous, carrying wisdom that arrives before the logic does. She appears repeatedly in your planetary aspects (sextile Venus, Mars, Uranus, Neptune; trine Pluto) — she is in relationship with everything in your chart.

Major Arcana · XIX
The Sun
Sun in Aquarius · 1st House · conjunct Mercury + Saturn
Your Sun Card
Vitality, clarity, joy, authentic self-expression. The Sun card illuminates what is true. In conjunction with Mercury (The Magician) and Saturn (The World), your solar energy is both brilliantly communicative and structurally disciplined — light that builds.
Your Sun square Pluto (Judgement Rx) creates tension between illumination and transformation — the part of you that wants to shine openly versus the part that does its most powerful work in the dark. Both are real. Both are you.
I
Major Arcana · I
The Magician
Mercury in Aquarius · 1st House · conjunct Sun + Saturn
Your Mercury Card
The master of will, skill, and the power to translate divine impulse into earthly action. The Magician has all four suits on his table — all elements, all tools — and knows how to use them. "As above, so below."
Mercury conjunct Saturn in your 1st house gives your Magician unusual discipline and longevity. You don't just have ideas — you can build with them. Your words carry structural weight. The Magician conjunct The World (Saturn) means your craft becomes your legacy.

Major Arcana · III
The Empress
Venus in Capricorn · 12th House
Your Venus Card
Abundance, beauty, sensuality, creative fertility, the body as sacred. The Empress creates — life, art, beauty, love — from her very nature. She does not try to be magnetic. She simply is.
Venus in your 12th house places The Empress in the hidden realm — your beauty, magnetism, and creative abundance operate largely below the surface, often more felt by others than claimed by you. The 12th house stellium is your hidden garden. The Empress tends it privately.
XVI
Major Arcana · XVI
The Tower
Mars in Capricorn · 12th House
Your Mars Card
Sudden revelation, structural collapse of what was built on false foundation, liberation through disruption. The Tower is not destruction for its own sake — it is the universe insisting that what cannot stand must not stand.
Mars in your 12th means The Tower operates internally and often invisibly — transformations that others don't see coming because they were never watching where they happened. Mars sextile Pluto means your Tower moments feed Judgement. Every collapse is composted.
XX
Major Arcana · XX
Judgement
Pluto in Scorpio · conjunct MC · 10th House
Your Pluto + MC Card
The call to rise. The soul's reckoning. The final transformation before wholeness. Judgement is not punishment — it is the moment you answer the call you have been hearing your whole life and stop pretending you didn't hear it.
Pluto conjunct MC is one of the most powerful destiny placements in astrology. Judgement on your MC means your public calling IS transformation — yours, and others'. You are here to answer the call loudly enough that others remember they have one too.

Major Arcana · 0
The Fool
Uranus in Capricorn · 12th House
Your Uranus Card
Pure potential. The leap before the landing. The one who walks off the cliff not from ignorance but from trust. The Fool carries everything they need in a small bundle — the universe is their safety net.
Uranus in your 12th means The Fool's leaps happen in hidden, liminal spaces — private revolutions that precede the public ones. The Fool conjunct The Hanged Man (Neptune) and The Empress (Venus) in your 12th creates a triad of hidden liberation, surrender, and abundance.
XII
Major Arcana · XII
The Hanged Man
Neptune in Capricorn · 12th House
Your Neptune Card
Sacred suspension. The wisdom that comes only through surrender. The Hanged Man is not stuck — he is choosing to pause, to see from a different angle, to let go of the need to act and instead to receive. The upside-down perspective is the gift.
Neptune in your 12th house is The Hanged Man in his most natural habitat — the liminal, the dissolving, the between. Your greatest spiritual insights come through surrender, not striving. This is also your psychic sensitivity: the veil is thinner for you than for most.
XXI
Major Arcana · XXI
The World
Saturn in Aquarius · 1st House · conjunct Sun + Mercury
Your Saturn Card
Completion, wholeness, mastery, the culmination of a long cycle. The World dancer moves freely within the wreath — the discipline has become freedom. What was once effort is now expression.
Saturn (The World) conjunct your Sun (The Sun) and Mercury (The Magician) in your 1st house means completion, mastery, and disciplined expression are written into your identity. You are not building toward The World — you were born into the lesson of it. Capricorn rising reinforces this: your chart ruler IS The World.
X
Major Arcana · X · Reversed
Wheel of Fortune (Rx)
Jupiter Rx in Virgo · 7th House
Your Jupiter Card
Rx: Fortune that turns inward. Expansion through introspection. The luck that is found not outside but within. Jupiter Rx asks you to be your own source of abundance before seeking it in the external world.
Jupiter Rx in your 7th house means The Wheel turns inward specifically around partnership and relationships — your greatest expansion comes through deep, chosen partnerships rather than broad social networks. The luck is intimate, not public.
XI
Major Arcana · XI · Life Path
Justice
Life Path 11 · Master Number
Your Life Path Card
Truth, cause and effect, clarity of vision, divine law. Justice holds the sword of discernment and the scales of perfect balance — she sees without bias and acts without hesitation once she knows what is true.
Life Path 11 reduces to 2 (Justice/XI = 1+1=2 in some systems, though traditionally 11 maps to Justice at XI). The combination of 11's illumination with Justice's clarity means your life path is literally to see truth clearly and transmit it. The scales in Justice's hands are yours to hold.
XVII
Major Arcana · XVII
The Star
Sun in Aquarius · Tropical Zodiac
Your Sun Sign Card
Hope, renewal, divine connection, pouring yourself out in service to something larger. The Star pours endlessly from both vessels — one into the earth, one back into water — she does not run dry because she is connected to the source.
Aquarius is The Star — the water-bearer who is not water but air, who carries what nourishes others but is not defined by it. Your Sun in Aquarius makes you the one who holds the vision of what could be even when no one else can see it yet. The Star's water is hope. You carry it.
