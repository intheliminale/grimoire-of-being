Your Lunar Signature

Born on a New Moon
February 6, 1992 · Moon in Pisces · 2nd House

You were born under a New Moon — the darkest night of the lunar cycle, the moment of inception before any light is visible. This is not a subtle placement. Being born on a New Moon means your entire life operates on New Moon energy: you are an initiator, a beginner of cycles, someone who is always in some way starting.

New Moon people are seeds, not flowers. They are beginnings looking for their becoming. The world often doesn't see them clearly because they are still emerging — but they are always moving toward something, even when they cannot yet name it.

Your New Moon lands in Pisces in the 2nd house, which adds layers of significance. The 2nd house governs what you value, what you call your own, and your sense of security and self-worth. A Pisces New Moon here means your instinct for self-worth is deeply intuitive, fluid, and spiritually oriented — you know your value through feeling rather than evidence, through resonance rather than proof.

Every New Moon in Pisces is personal for you. It returns to the degree of your birth each year and activates your natal lunar signature. These are your most potent months for new beginnings, intention-setting, and initiating anything that matters.

Your Moon in Pisces is The High Priestess — she does not show you everything at once. She reveals by degree, through feeling, through the liminal space between waking and sleep. She is most powerful at thresholds: the moment before dawn, the space between one cycle and the next, the dark moon before the new.

Annual Power Window — New Moon in Pisces
Your most potent new beginning window each year
Every year when the New Moon returns to Pisces (typically late February or early March), it activates your natal Moon placement and amplifies your initiator energy tenfold. This is your personal lunar new year — more significant for you than January 1st or even the standard astrological new year at Aries.
What to do
Set your most significant intentions for the year. Begin creative projects. Initiate spiritual practices. Make decisions you have been preparing for. This window is yours.
How to honor it
Florida water, ocean water, rainbow moonstone, amethyst. Pre-dawn ritual. Written intentions in water (dissolving paper). Dream journaling in the days before and after.
